# Code of Conduct

Be precise, be kind, and do not waste other people's time.

- No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- No posting of private keys, seeds, or personal data.
- Demo agents in this repo are fictional. Do not present them as live operators.
- Legal-category capabilities are research aids, not legal advice — do not market them otherwise.
- Assume good faith on protocol debates; back claims with a test or a spec patch.

Maintainers may reject contributions that violate these rules or the security policy.
